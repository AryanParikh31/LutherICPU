🧸 The 10-Year-Old Mental Model

How we transform flat photographs into continuous, photorealistic 3D simulations.

01
🔍

The Photo Detective

Epipolar Geometry & Feature Matching

"Imagine taking 100 photos of your bedroom. The software looks at them like a detective finding matching clues (like the corner of your desk) to figure out exactly where you were standing for every photo."

We detect gradient keypoints across your photos, solve the 8-point camera alignment equations using RANSAC, and triangulate 3D point cloud seeds via Direct Linear Transform (DLT).

02
✨

The Magic Flat Stickers

Planar 2D Micro-Surfels & Anti-Aliasing

"Instead of stacking rigid blocks like Minecraft, the computer places millions of microscopic, shiny oval stickers over everything. On a bicycle spoke, they stretch into skinny needles; on a table, they lay completely flat. Because their edges melt together, you see a solid, real world instead of dots."

Each planar surfel stores a 3D position $\mathbf{\mu}$, tangent vectors $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}$, and explicit surface normal $\mathbf{n}$. Mip-filtering ($\mathbf{\Sigma}' + 0.3 I$) prevents aliasing.

03
🦎

The Chameleon Glare

Spherical Harmonics (View-Dependent Radiance)

"Each sticker remembers how light bounces off it. When you walk past a shiny window, the sticker changes color to create real sun reflections."

Directional radiance is encoded in Degree 3 Legendre polynomial coefficients (48 floats per surfel), dynamically re-evaluated as your virtual camera navigates through the scene.

04
🛡️

The Invisible Bumper

The Boundary Collision Cage

"An invisible bubble cushion surrounds you inside the room so you cannot fall through the floor or wander into empty black space."

A 3D convex hull with inward safety margins constantly cushions camera coordinates via clamp_camera(), preventing unmapped voids or glitchy clipping.
